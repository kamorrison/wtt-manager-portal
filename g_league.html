WTT G League Rules

4 Eligible Players

Designate up to four players from the current or immediately preceding WTT rookie draft class.

2 G League Spots

Only two designated players may be actively assigned to the G League at once.

Eligible Can Stay Active

Eligibility does not use a G League spot. An eligible player may remain on the active roster.

Protection Limit

A third active assignment is invalid and may leave the extra player exposed under league rules.
